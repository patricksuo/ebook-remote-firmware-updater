# Updating a Hanlinyue (翰林阅) Free2 from your phone

This is **Hanlinyue's official guide for the Free2**, translated. **I don't own this remote, so I haven't verified
any of it myself.** The Mac app can't update Hanlinyue remotes: they use a different chip (Jieli, 杰理) and a different
updater.

Hanlinyue's original page, with the firmware downloads: [hanlinyue.com.cn/1782274267396](https://hanlinyue.com.cn/1782274267396).
They also have a [video guide](https://cdn.hanlinyue.com.cn/OTA.mp4) (Chinese, about 80 MB).

## 1. Install the 杰理OTA升级 app

- **iPhone / iPad:** search the App Store for `杰理OTA升级` or `Jieli OTA`
  ([App Store link](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1535431659)). It's free, by *Zhuhai Jieli Technology Co., Ltd*,
  and needs iOS 12 or later.
- **Android:** Hanlinyue says to get it from your phone's app store. I couldn't find it on Google Play.

## 2. Download the Free2 firmware

Open [Hanlinyue's firmware page](https://hanlinyue.com.cn/1782274267396) on your phone and download the **Free2**
file. There are two: **Free2** and **Free2 (2026 edition)**; pick the one that matches your remote.

## 3. Open the file in the app

Tap the downloaded firmware file. In the menu that pops up choose **Open with… / other apps**,
then **杰理OTA升级**.

## 4. Connect the remote

1. Turn on the phone's Bluetooth and switch the remote on.
2. **If the remote has been paired with this phone before, forget it first** in the phone's Bluetooth settings.
   Also make sure it isn't connected to your Kindle or another device.
3. In the app's connect page, find your remote's name and tap it to connect. A **✓** appears after the name when it's
   connected.

## 5. Install

1. Tap **升级 (Upgrade)**, choose the firmware file you downloaded and start the upgrade.
2. The app shows, in order: checking the file → connecting → upgrading → upgrade complete.
3. Tap OK, then **switch the remote OFF and ON**.

Before pairing it with your Kindle, forget the remote in the phone's Bluetooth settings, so the phone doesn't connect
to it again first. On the Kindle: *Settings › Bluetooth*.
